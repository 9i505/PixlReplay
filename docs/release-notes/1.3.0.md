Pixl Replay 1.3.0: replay on the disk or in RAM, a sound track per app, cloud saving, clearer clips.

- Instant replay on your disk (SSD or HDD) or in RAM, up to 20 minutes, and if you like a fresh start after each save.
- A sound track per app: a mixer with a row per source (game, Discord, music, microphone, any app or device), each with its own level and, if you like, its own track; the editor sets each track's level.
- Save to OneDrive, Dropbox or Google Drive, and free the local copies if you like.
- Camera: your webcam or phone in a corner (square or round) or above a 9:16 picture, and if you like saved on its own.
- Up to five apps in Apps mode (the one in front is recorded), and games picked up in Full screen too.
- Automatic quality: Pixl Replay picks the encoder, size, frame rate and quality for your PC, and steps down by itself if frames are skipped or a game falls behind.
- Clearer clips: Ultra and High use the encoder's best settings while the graphics card has room (up to 1080p at 170 fps), and a clip shared to a size limit (Discord) lowers its size or frame rate sooner, so it stays sharp instead of blurry.
- Smart Save: choosing its microphone no longer freezes the settings window, and it works with separate sound sources too.
- Checks for updates from the General page, and many fixes.
- More screens, the Look page and keys that work in games all stay, now on the new engine.
