Pixl Replay 1.3.3: high frame rates in games, up to 600 fps at 1080p and 1000 at 720p.

- Frame rates past your screen's: 360, 500, 600 and 1000 fps for games, which give those frames (the desktop stays at your screen's rate). The list shows what your graphics card keeps up with at the chosen size.
- High frame rates no longer stutter: past 240 fps the encoder picks a faster setting by itself, so every frame is kept. Before, a 360 fps clip repeated most of its frames.

فريمات عالية في الألعاب: 360 و500 و600 فريم على 1080p، و1000 على 720p، بدون تقطيع. القائمة تعرض اللي تقدر عليه كرت الشاشة بالدقة اللي اخترتها.
