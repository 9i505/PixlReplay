Pixl Replay 1.2.1: keys and voice that work in games.

- Keys that work in games: NVIDIA's overlay takes Alt+F10 and Alt+F9 once a game starts, so those keys did nothing there. Pixl Replay now avoids NVIDIA's shortcuts and moves yours once, with a notice: save is Alt+F8, recording Alt+F7 (change them any time on the Hotkeys page).
- Smart Save hears your real microphone: when the recording takes a virtual one (VB-Cable, Voicemeeter, Sonar), Smart Save listens to your actual microphone, so your phrase is recognised. Pick another on the Smart Save page. Record your phrase again once after updating.

اختصارات تشتغل داخل الألعاب (بعيدًا عن اختصارات NVIDIA)، والحفظ الصوتي يسمع ميكروفونك الحقيقي.
